Who we are

Engineered for reliability.

Maple Lodge Farms · substation maintenance

A licensed team of journeymen, engineers, apprentices and power systems specialists working on medium and high voltage equipment.

From emergency response on a failing transformer to the engineering study that keeps it from failing again, ISCOCA carries projects from start to finish. A large fleet of electrical test equipment supports advanced testing and analysis on power systems throughout Canada.

About ISCOCA
