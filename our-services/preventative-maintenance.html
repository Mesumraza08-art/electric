Preventative maintenance.

Scheduled service that catches the problem before it becomes an outage.

What is PM?

Scheduled, not reactive.

Reactive maintenance is typically an emergency response to equipment failure. Preventative maintenance is the regularly scheduled service of equipment, with intervals that depend on the demands of the electrical system.

Our maintenance clients are corporations with medium to large operations and equipment rated 600V and above. Our scope is comprehensive and follows the best industrial practices set in North America, such as NETA, with customized programs built around your system.

Reactive maintenance

The IEEE lists these disadvantages

  • Safety issues
  • Uncontrollable and unpredictable budget costs
  • Shorter life expectancy for equipment
  • Greater chance of inspection failure
  • Increased downtime for equipment and employees
  • Repeat issues and breakdowns
