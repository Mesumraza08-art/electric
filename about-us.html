Who we are

A full-service power systems team.

Team Iscoca is a collaborative, experienced group of journeymen, engineers, apprentices and other power systems specialists.

We are a licensed electrical contractor with the Electrical Safety Authority (ESA). We perform a range of services on medium to high voltage equipment, from emergency response to maintenance and testing.

Iscoca carries a large collection of electrical test equipment, and our technicians carry out advanced testing and analysis on power systems throughout Canada.

Being full-service lets us carry projects from start to finish, whether that is a custom build or a commissioning project.
